Building layouts that survive real content

Most layouts look fine with the text a designer chose. They break when a title runs three lines or a price field holds a much longer number. Designing against awkward content early saves rework later.

Grid and Flexbox both help here, but for different reasons. Grid defines the page's overall skeleton; Flexbox handles how a row of related items shares the space it is given.
