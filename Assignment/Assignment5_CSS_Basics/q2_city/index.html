Kyoto, Japan

Overview Temples Food Travel Tips

Overview

Kyoto was the imperial capital of Japan for over a thousand years and remains its cultural heart, known for wooden temples, geisha districts, and quiet gardens tucked between modern streets.

Temples & Shrines

Fushimi Inari's thousands of vermilion torii gates and Kinkaku-ji's golden pavilion make Kyoto one of the most photographed cities in Asia.

Food Culture

From kaiseki multi-course dinners to street-side yatsuhashi sweets, Kyoto's food scene blends centuries-old tradition with everyday charm.

Travel Tips

Rent a bicycle to explore the Arashiyama bamboo grove, and visit temples early morning to avoid the crowds.
